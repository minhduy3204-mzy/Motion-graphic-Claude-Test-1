# Rocket Studio / Design with direction

An invented design application for this requested film, associated visually with Rocket Group's website project. This is a concept identity, not a claim that Rocket Group sells an existing software product.

## Style Prompt
A precise, tactile creative workspace. Warm ivory application chrome frames a deep-green web artboard. One living document: select Hero / Title, change its weight and acid-yellow fill, inspect the improved site, open Preview, and carry the artboard's directional geometry into a confident app lockup. Typography is large and assured. Thin rules, clear handles and quiet property controls make every action legible. Depth comes from a shallow camera orbit around the same workspace and subtle Z separation of its real layers. The final composition rests for more than two seconds.

## Typography
Manrope variable, weights350/500/800: deliberate contrast between the initial thin title and its stronger designed state. IBM Plex Mono400/500: measurements and property values. Both are licensed and bundled. Headlines at84–144px, UI labels20px+ wherever practical; 18px only for peripheral rulers. Data uses tabular numbers.

## Motion rules
A single deterministic timeline drives all CSS visual variables, Remotion frames, HyperFrames GSAP tracks and audio. Cursor arrives, briefly rests, presses, and produces the correct selected/edited/preview state. Camera keeps shallow perspective and settles during important reads. Object identity, layer labels and selected measurements come from one document config. Related movement overlaps; a final canvas expansion is the transition to the app lockup.

## What NOT to Do
- No invented performance statistics, customer badges or real-product claims.
- No disconnected floating cards, arbitrary dashboards or changing layer identities.
- No decorative random numbers, tiny dense metadata, excessive glow or glitches.
- No UI response before its cursor click, unexplained property changes or camera whips.
- No reuse of the supplied Strata footage or its purple identity.
