## Typography and font licenses

The live site loads **Inter Tight**, while the final `rocket-font.css` cascade forces **“Helvetica Neue”, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif** on most main content. Footer/modal styling can retain Inter Tight. On this Linux capture host, the Helvetica/Arial stack resolves to **Liberation Sans**. The actual-site PNGs preserve that captured appearance.

The film deliberately uses the site's available **Inter Tight** consistently for editable motion typography. This is a motion-design choice, not a claim that every live heading currently renders in Inter Tight. **Liberation Sans** is bundled as an editable alternative/fallback; proprietary Helvetica or Arial font files are not bundled.

- `fonts/inter-tight-latin-variable.woff2`: Inter Tight, from `@fontsource-variable/inter-tight` 5.3.0; SIL Open Font License 1.1. Preserve `fonts/INTER-TIGHT-LICENSE.txt` when redistributing.
- `fonts/liberation-sans-regular.ttf` and `fonts/liberation-sans-bold.ttf`: Liberation Fonts; SIL Open Font License 1.1. Preserve `fonts/LIBERATION-LICENSE.txt` when redistributing.
